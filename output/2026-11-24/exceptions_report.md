## Root causes and upstream fixes

### Missing paperwork (8 exceptions: SJ-1002, SJ-1007, SJ-1039, SJ-1011, SJ-1025, SJ-1036, SJ-1005, SJ-1016)
- Pattern: post-commissioning paperwork (G98 notification, MCS, handover) is running late, and two jobs (SJ-1025, SJ-1036, both Team C) were marked complete with documents missing. One G99 application (SJ-1005) has not gone in, and one job sheet (SJ-1016) was never submitted. Both MCS items (SJ-1007, SJ-1039) are Team B jobs. Ask whether they are waiting on test results from the team; the data doesn't show this.
- Upstream fix: on the commissioning date, automatically create G98/G99, MCS and handover tasks with due dates, and block the "complete" status until the documents index holds every required document for that job type.

### Supplier (3 exceptions: SJ-1008, SJ-1004, SJ-1019)
- Pattern: Brightfield billed two lines above the agreed price (8% and 5%). This suggests they are billing at list price, or our price list is out of date. Kestrel re-billed a scaffold line already on an earlier invoice. Brightfield is also on 2 of the "Unclear" invoice items (SJ-1140, SJ-1031), so it accounts for 4 of the 5 invoice exceptions.
- Upstream fix: send the agreed price list with every PO, and match every invoice against the PO, the price list and the install record before approving it for payment. Reject mismatches automatically.

### Data entry (4 exceptions: SJ-1003, SJ-1030, SJ-1033, SJ-1021)
- Pattern: tracker and installation records disagree on count, size and date, or a date is blank. Three of these four are Team B jobs (SJ-1003, SJ-1030, SJ-1033).
- Upstream fix: calculate kWp in the tracker from panels × watts instead of typing it, make stage dates mandatory, and have the installer job sheet completed on a phone at commissioning so the two records are filled in from the same source.

### Scheduling (1 exception: SJ-1018)
- Pattern: scaffold booked on 19 Oct with no install 36 days later.
- Upstream fix: hold a weekly look-ahead of jobs near their stage limit, and book scaffold only once an install date is confirmed.

### External (DNO) (1 exception: SJ-1012)
- Pattern: G99 approval outstanding 71 days after submission.
- Upstream fix: record the DNO reference against each job, chase at a set interval, and escalate at the limit.

### Unclear (6 exceptions: SJ-1027, SJ-1014, SJ-1023, SJ-1009, SJ-1140, SJ-1031)
- Pattern: mostly equipment that differs from the tracker on completed jobs (SJ-1009 Team A, SJ-1023 Team A, SJ-1014 Team C), plus one date order on a G99 job, one unknown job reference and one panel over-supply.
- Upstream fix: any on-site substitution must be approved by Design and logged in the tracker before the team leaves site, with a check of the DNO route when inverter or battery size changes.

### Cross-cutting pattern: Team B
