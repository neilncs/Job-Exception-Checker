## Root causes and upstream fixes

### Missing paperwork (7 exceptions: SJ-1002, SJ-1007, SJ-1039, SJ-1011, SJ-1025, SJ-1036, SJ-1005)
- Pattern: post-commissioning admin (G98 notification, MCS, handover, warranty registration, EIC filing) is not getting done on time, and the backlog is growing. Today's only new item is another overdue MCS certificate. Both overdue MCS items (SJ-1007, SJ-1039) are Team B jobs, which may mean the office is waiting on something from Team B. The data does not show that, so check it before blaming either side. Two jobs (SJ-1025, SJ-1036) were closed as complete without a full document set.
- Upstream fix: create due-dated tasks for the G98/G99 notification, MCS, handover and warranty registrations automatically on the commissioning date, and block the tracker from moving a job to `complete` until the documents index holds the full set for that job type.

### Supplier (5 exceptions: SJ-1008, SJ-1004, SJ-1019, SJ-1031, plus SJ-1140 on hold)
- Pattern: four of the five invoice exceptions are from Brightfield Wholesale Ltd: two prices above agreed (BFW-24115, BFW-24164), two surplus panels on SJ-1031, and one invoice for an unknown job (BFW-24257). Brightfield may be billing at list price rather than the agreed price, so check that our price list is current. Separately, Kestrel re-billed a scaffold already invoiced (KSL-5328 vs KSL-5324).
- Upstream fix: send the agreed price list with each PO, and match every invoice line to the PO, the job and the installed quantity before approving payment.

### Data entry: installer job sheets (4 exceptions: SJ-1003, SJ-1033, SJ-1016, and possibly SJ-1027)
- Pattern: three are Team B jobs (SJ-1003 count, SJ-1033 date, SJ-1027 dates). SJ-1016 has no job sheet at all. SJ-1027's dates may be a data-entry error rather than a breach.
- Upstream fix: have the job sheet completed on a phone at commissioning, before the team leaves site, with the tracker's planned kit and dates pre-filled for confirmation.

### Data entry: office (2 exceptions: SJ-1030, SJ-1021)
- Pattern: a typed kWp that does not match the kit (3.40 vs 4.30), and a blank survey date.
- Upstream fix: calculate kWp in the tracker from panel count x panel wattage, and make stage dates mandatory when a stage is set.

### Scheduling (1 exception: SJ-1018)
- Pattern: the scaffold was booked but no install has followed after 36 days. The scaffold may be costing hire while it stands.
- Upstream fix: hold a weekly look-ahead of jobs near their stage limit, and book scaffold only once an install date is confirmed.

### External (DNO) (1 exception: SJ-1012)
- Pattern: a commercial G99 approval has been outstanding for 71 days.
- Upstream fix: record the DNO reference in the tracker, chase at a set interval (e.g. every 14 days), and escalate at the limit.

### Unclear: possible kit substitutions (3 exceptions: SJ-1009, SJ-1014, SJ-1023)
- Pattern: the panel, inverter and battery models differ between the tracker and the install record on completed jobs (Teams A, C and A).
- Upstream fix: add a substitution approval step (Design sign-off) that updates the tracker, design and DNO paperwork before an installer fits different kit.
